# Privacy Policy - DON'T TRUST

**Last updated: 5 November 2026**

DON'T TRUST ("the game") is a narrative thriller game published by **AppVita Studio**.
This policy explains what data the game handles and why.

## 1. Your game data stays on your device

Your progress, saves, settings, unlocked endings and statistics are stored **only
on your device**, in the game's private storage. We do not collect them, we do not
transmit them and we have no servers that receive them. There is no account and
no registration.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and is supported by advertising provided by **Google AdMob**.
To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

- the **advertising identifier** of your device (Android Advertising ID)
- technical data such as device model, operating system version, language and
  **approximate location derived from your IP address**
- data about interaction with the ads shown

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent data controller,
according to its own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- AdMob and user consent: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

No game data (saves, choices, settings) is shared with the advertising network.

## 3. Consent (EU / EEA / UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the
game shows you a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform **before any
ad is loaded**. You choose whether to allow personalised advertising. If consent
is required and not given, no ads are requested at all.

You can change your choice at any time from **Settings > Privacy options** inside
the game.

## 4. Permissions

- **Internet and network state**: used only to load ads and the consent form.
  The story itself works offline.
- **Vibration**: optional haptic feedback, can be turned off in Settings.

## 5. Data we do not collect

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos,
precise location or any content of your play. We use no analytics SDK of our own.

## 6. Children

The game is not directed to children. It is intended for users aged 18 and over.

## 7. Your rights

Since your game data never leaves your device, you are in full control of it:

- **Deletion**: use Settings > Reset progress, or uninstall the game to remove
  everything
- **Advertising**: reset or delete your advertising ID from the Android settings,
  and change your consent from Settings > Privacy options

For advertising data processed by Google, you can exercise your rights directly
with Google through the links in section 2.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top of this page changes
with it, and the new version is published at this same address.
